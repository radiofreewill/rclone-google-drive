```html Privacy Policy - Rclone Google Drive
Privacy Policy

Rclone Google Drive - Personal Integration

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Purpose

This application is a personal integration that uses rclone to access the owner's Google Drive account.

Google User Data

The application requests Google Drive access to allow its owner to browse, read, create, modify, synchronize, and manage files in their own Google Drive.

Data Storage and Sharing

Google Drive files are accessed through rclone. Authentication credentials are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration.

The application operator does not operate a separate server that collects Google Drive data. No Google user data is sold or shared with advertisers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Files downloaded to the owner's computer remain under the owner's control. Local copies and authentication credentials can be deleted by the owner.

Access Revocation

The owner can revoke this application's access at https://myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact

For inquiries, contact the repository owner through https://github.com/radiofreewill/rclone-google-drive.

```
